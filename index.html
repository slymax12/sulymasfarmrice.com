Our Processing Methods

Remove Black Seed

Advanced sorting technology removes any black or discolored seeds to ensure premium quality rice grains.

Step 1

Remove Stones

Stone extraction process using specialized machinery guarantees 100% stone-free rice for safe consumption.

Step 2

Water Polish

Premium water polishing gives the rice a brilliant white shine and smooth texture for perfect cooking.

Step 3

Grading

Precise grading separates rice by size and quality to ensure uniform cooking and consistent taste.

Step 4

Milling

State-of-the-art milling technology removes husks and bran while preserving maximum nutrition and flavor.
